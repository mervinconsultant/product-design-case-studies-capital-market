# Groww — Improving Instrument Discovery with Quick Watchlist Actions

### UX Audit · Fintech · Investing

## Overview

Groww is an investing platform used by retail investors to discover
and invest in stocks, mutual funds, indices and other financial
instruments.

This UX audit focused on friction in the way users save instruments
while browsing listing screens.

## The Challenge

Users exploring multiple instruments could not bookmark or add items
to their watchlist directly from the listing screen.

They had to open each instrument's detail page before adding it to
their watchlist, interrupting the browsing and comparison flow.

### Observed Problems

- No quick-save option from the browse or listing screen
- Extra taps required to add watchlist items
- Interrupted browsing and comparison flow
- Slower discovery workflow for active investors

## Core Insight

> During browsing, user intent is often a quick scan followed by a
> quick save.

Requiring users to navigate to a detail page breaks that momentum and
adds friction at a common interaction point.

## Design Principle

> **Support user intent at the moment it occurs.**

## Proposed Solution

Add a bookmark star icon or **+ Watchlist** CTA directly at the
instrument row level.

This allows users to save an instrument instantly without leaving
the current listing screen.

## Before → After

**Before**

Open instrument → Navigate to detail page → Add to watchlist

**After**

Browse screen → Tap star → Saved instantly

## Expected Impact

- 35% faster watchlist additions
- 25% increase in watchlist feature usage
- Reduced drop-off during discovery journeys
- Higher repeat sessions from active users

## Why This Works

- Faster decision-making during browsing sessions
- Easier comparison of multiple instruments
- Improved efficiency for frequent traders
- Better engagement with the watchlist feature

## Method

**UX Audit · Heuristic Analysis · Task Analysis**

The audit focused on identifying micro-frictions in the instrument
discovery and watchlist workflow and proposing a focused interaction
improvement.
