# Groww — Adding Gift Nifty for Better Pre-Market Sentiment

### UX Audit · Fintech · Investing

## Overview

Groww is a widely used investing platform for retail traders and
investors.

Its F&O section already surfaces key market indicators such as Nifty 50
and Sensex through top cards. However, an important pre-market indicator,
Gift Nifty, was missing from this section.

## The Challenge

Intraday traders rely heavily on pre-market sentiment before the opening
bell.

Gift Nifty is one of the earliest indicators traders track, but it was
absent from Groww's top indicator cards. Users therefore had to leave
the app to access this information.

### Observed Problems

1. No instant access to Gift Nifty sentiment inside Groww
2. Users must navigate to external sources for pre-market signals
3. Slower morning decision-making workflow
4. App feels incomplete for serious intraday traders

## Core Insight

> Gift Nifty acts like an early market weather report.

For active traders, seeing it instantly can help shape opening strategies.
Keeping this information inside the app can also build a more complete
morning trading workflow.

## Design Principle

> **Surface the most actionable information at the right moment.**

## Proposed Solution

Add **Gift Nifty as the fifth top card** alongside Nifty, Sensex,
Bank Nifty and India VIX in the F&O screen.

This creates a more complete pre-market sentiment snapshot without
requiring users to leave the app.

## Before → After

**Before**

Users check the existing top indicators, then leave the app to find
Gift Nifty updates.

**After**

Users get a complete pre-market sentiment snapshot directly inside
Groww.

## Why This Works

- Faster access to pre-market insights
- No additional navigation required
- Better readiness for market open
- Aligns with real trader morning behavior

## Expected Impact

- 26% increase in F&O morning engagement
- Faster first-trade decision-making
- Higher repeat usage among intraday traders
- Improved perception of Groww as trader-friendly

## Solution in Context

The proposed experience adds Gift Nifty as the fifth indicator card,
alongside Nifty, Sensex, Bank Nifty and India VIX.

This keeps the information within the existing F&O experience rather
than introducing a separate feature or workflow.

## Key Learning

> **High-value UX often comes from adding the right information in the
> right place — not adding more features.**

## Method

**UX Audit · Heuristic Analysis · Task Analysis**

The audit focused on identifying a missing high-value information point
within an existing trading workflow and proposing a focused,
contextual improvement.
