# Groww — Improving Instrument Discovery with Quick Watchlist Actions

### UX Audit · Fintech · Investing

## Overview

Groww is a popular investing platform used by retail investors to
discover and invest in stocks, mutual funds, indices and other
financial instruments.

This UX audit focused on friction in how users save instruments while
browsing listing screens.

## The Challenge

Users exploring multiple instruments could not bookmark or add items
to their watchlist directly from the listing screen.

They had to first open each instrument's detail page before adding it
to their watchlist, interrupting the browsing and comparison flow.

### Observed Problems

1. No quick-save option from the browse or listing screen
2. Extra taps required to add watchlist items
3. Interrupted browsing and comparison flow
4. Slower discovery workflow for active investors

## Core Insight

> During browsing, user intent is often a quick scan followed by a
> quick save.

Requiring detail-page navigation breaks browsing momentum and adds
friction at the most common interaction point.

## Design Principle

> **Support user intent at the moment it occurs.**

## Proposed Solution

Add a bookmark star icon or **+ Watchlist** CTA directly at the
instrument row level.

This allows users to save instruments instantly without leaving the
current listing screen.

## Before → After

**Before**

Open instrument → Navigate to detail page → Add to watchlist

**After**

Browse screen → Tap star → Saved instantly

## Why This Works

- Faster decision-making during browsing sessions
- Easier comparison of multiple instruments
- Improved efficiency for frequent traders
- Better engagement with the watchlist feature

## Expected Impact

- 35% faster watchlist additions
- 25% increase in watchlist feature usage
- Reduced drop-off during discovery journeys
- Higher repeat sessions from active users

## Method

**UX Audit · Heuristic Analysis · Task Analysis**

The audit focused on identifying friction in the instrument discovery
and watchlist workflow and proposing a focused interaction improvement.
