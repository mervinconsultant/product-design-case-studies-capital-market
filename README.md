## Case Studies

### Delta Exchange — Faster Access to the Markets That Matter

Fintech · Crypto Trading · UX Audit

A UX audit of Delta Exchange focused on the watchlist experience.

The audit identified friction caused by starred assets being scattered
across multiple tabs, requiring traders to repeatedly switch tabs
to access their preferred markets.

The proposed solution was a dedicated **Favorites** tab that brings
all starred assets into one persistent view.

Focus

- UX Audit
- Heuristic Analysis
- Task Analysis
- Information Architecture
- Trading Workflows
- Interaction Design

**Key Outcome**

A simpler watchlist structure that reduces navigation friction and
gives traders faster access to their most-watched markets.
