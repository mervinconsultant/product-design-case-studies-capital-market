# Delta Exchange — Dedicated Favorites Tab

### UX Audit · Fintech · Crypto Trading

## Overview

Delta Exchange is a crypto derivatives trading platform where traders
monitor multiple assets across different market categories.

This UX audit focused on friction in the existing Watchlist experience
and explored how traders could access their preferred markets faster.

## The Challenge

The existing Watchlist experience did not provide a dedicated Favorites
view. Starred assets were scattered across multiple tabs, forcing traders
to repeatedly switch tabs to track their preferred markets.

### Observed Problems

- Starred assets were not surfaced in a single dedicated view
- Repeated tab switching interrupted the trading workflow
- Additional navigation increased friction for active traders
- There was no at-a-glance Favorites snapshot

## Core Insight

> Traders need instant access to their most-watched assets.

Every unnecessary tap or navigation step adds cognitive load during
time-sensitive trading decisions.

## Design Principle

> **Reduce steps between intent and information.**

## Proposed Solution

Introduce a dedicated **Favorites** tab alongside the existing **All**
tab.

Any starred asset would automatically appear in the Favorites view,
giving traders a persistent, zero-effort way to access their most-watched
markets.

## Before → After

**Before**

Starred assets were mixed across multiple tabs, requiring repeated
navigation to find preferred markets.

**After**

A dedicated Favorites tab surfaces all starred assets in one place.

## Expected Impact

- Faster access to tracked markets
- Reduced friction in the daily trading workflow
- Improved session efficiency
- Better alignment with professional trader behavior
- Higher engagement with the Watchlist feature

## Method

**Heuristic Analysis + Task Analysis**

The audit focused on identifying friction within the trading workflow
and proposing a focused improvement rather than redesigning the entire
product.
